# Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: 2024**

1. **Data We Collect**: 
   - Authentication data via Google/Firebase.
   - Gameplay data (guesses, streaks, scores).
   - Payment data is handled securely by Razorpay; we do not store your credit card details.
2. **How We Use Data**:
   - To maintain your streak and leaderboard rankings.
   - To manage your subscription status.
3. **Data Sharing**: We do not sell your personal data. Data is only shared with third-party processors like Firebase and Razorpay strictly for providing the service.
4. **Cookies**: We use local storage and cookies to save your game state and authentication session.
